### LISA (Lifetime ISA )

#### Contribution Rules

- Maximum £4,000 per tax year (counts towards your £20,000 ISA allowance).
- Government 25% bonus (up to £1,000/year).

#### Withdrawal Rules & Penalties

 - **Allowed without penalty:**

    - First home purchase (up to £450,000, after 12 months).
    - From age 60.
    - Terminal illness.

 - **Penalty:**

    - 25% withdrawal charge on other withdrawals (effectively losing the bonus plus some of your own money).
